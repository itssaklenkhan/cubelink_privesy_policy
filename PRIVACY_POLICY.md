# Privacy Policy for Tactile Cube Puzzle

**Effective Date:** September 29, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the **Tactile Cube Puzzle** app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created as a free ad-supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The Application is primarily an offline puzzle game. It does not require you to create an account, nor does it ask you to provide any personal data (such as your name, email, or phone number) directly to us. 

However, the Application does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, primarily for the purpose of serving advertisements and analyzing app performance.

### Local Storage
The Application stores game progress (such as unlocked levels, stars collected, and settings preferences) locally on your device's internal storage. We do not transmit or back up this data to any external servers. If you uninstall the Application or clear its data, this local progress will be permanently deleted.

### Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)
The Application uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. AdMob may use Advertising IDs and cookies to serve ads based on your interests and past interactions. 

Google AdMob may collect and process data such as:
- IP address
- Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
- Usage data and crash logs
- Network and diagnostic data

For more information on how Google uses this data and how you can opt-out of personalized advertising, please review [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 2. Permissions

The Application requires the following permissions to function correctly:
- **Internet & Network State:** Required exclusively to fetch and display third-party advertisements via Google AdMob.

## 3. Children's Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers (Note: we do not maintain servers, but this applies to any inadvertent data capture). If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## 4. Links to Other Sites

The Application may contain third-party advertisements that link to other sites. If you click on a third-party link (such as an ad banner), you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
